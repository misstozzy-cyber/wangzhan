1. **50+ True-Scale Silicone Neon Profiles**: Comprehensive geometry coverage — Top View (TV), Side View (SV), 360° Round (R13/R25/R40), 3D Dual-Bend, Dome, Dual-Side, Embedded/Recessed, Anti-Glare (UGR<19), IP68 Silicone & PU, Black Neon, AC230V Driverless, and Pixel DMX512/SPI.
2. **Complete Photometric Library (259 IES & LDT Sets)**: Every architectural neon series is backed by goniophotometer `.IES` / `.ldt` files and integrating sphere test reports — ready for DIALux and Relux lighting simulations.
3. **Industrial Manufacturing & Material Rigor**:
   - **Dow SILASTIC™ ET-7021** optical-grade silicone co-extrusion (UV, saltwater, solvent, and yellowing resistant).
   - **1-Bin LED selection** with finished-product **SDCM ≤ 3** color consistency and **CRI (Ra) ≥ 90 / 95**.
   - **160–180 lm/W High-Efficacy** LED strips, **COB PLUS** dot-free strips, and **48V Super-Long** strips (up to 60m single-feed run).
   - **11,000 m² across Two Shenzhen Factories** (F1 Shajing Antonio Technology Park, F2 Shiyan Shancheng Industrial Zone), 260 employees, 15 senior engineers, 6 silicone co-extrusion lines, and CNAS-grade in-house laboratory.
   - **Certifications**: ISO9001:2015, ISO14001, ISO45001:2018, ETL, RCM, TUV-CE, UKCA, RoHS, LM-80, ErP.
